Questions coaches ask

How much does it cost?+

Your first match is free. After that, ask us about single-match, league and season prices.

What video do we need?+

A full-match recording where you can see the ball. A phone on a tripod, a camera or Veo all work.

How fast is the report?+

Within 24 hours of receiving your video.

Can you come to our game?+

Yes. A matchday analyst tracks the game from your sideline and gives you the key stats at half-time.

Can we choose what you track?+

Yes. Tell us your team's targets and we'll build the report around them.
