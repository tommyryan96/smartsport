Who is ÉireMetrics for?

Our services are designed for Ladies GAA clubs of all levels, from underage teams to senior county squads, looking to make more informed coaching decisions using data.

What do I need to provide?

Simply provide your match footage after the game. We'll analyse the performance and deliver a professional report along with interactive dashboards.

Most reports are delivered within 48 hours, allowing coaching teams to review performances before the next training session.

Can reports be customised?

Yes. Reports can be tailored to focus on team KPIs, player development, opposition scouting or any performance indicators important to your coaching staff.
