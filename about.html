About

Proper analysis for the women's game

Top men's teams have analysts tracking every kickout and shot. Most Ladies football teams don't. ÉireMetrics brings that same detail to club and county teams, at a price that works for a club budget.

TR

Hi, I'm Tommy

I'm a data analyst based in Kildare, and I set up ÉireMetrics to give Ladies football teams the kind of analysis I work with every day.

I've spent more than five years working with data in Irish financial services. I have a degree in Computer Science and Software Engineering from Maynooth University and a Master's in Data Analytics from the National College of Ireland.

I built ÉireMetrics myself, from the tagging app to the reports and dashboards. That includes my own expected points model, which estimates how likely a shot is to score based on where it's taken from.
