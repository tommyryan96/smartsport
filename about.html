About Éire Metrics

Éire Metrics is a modern Irish sports analytics platform built to deliver clear, meaningful insights for players, coaches, clubs and supporters.

Starting with Gaelic football and expanding into soccer, Éire Metrics provides shot maps, expected points models, team comparisons and performance dashboards designed to be simple, fast and visually clear.

Why Éire Metrics?

  • Clear and easy-to-understand dashboards
  • Modern analytics approaches
  • Designed specifically for Irish sport
  • Accessible for clubs at every level
