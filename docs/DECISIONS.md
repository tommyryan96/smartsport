# EireMetrics Decisions Log

This document records important product and business decisions so we understand why they were made.

---

# 2026-07-22

## Decision 001
### Target Market

We will initially focus exclusively on Ladies GAA clubs.

Reason:
- Easier to become known in one niche.
- Less competition.
- Easier marketing.
- Build case studies before expanding.

Status: Active

---

## Decision 002
### Business Model

Offer services free to the first 3 clubs.

Reason:
- Build testimonials.
- Create portfolio.
- Validate reports.
- Gather feedback.

Status: Active

---

## Decision 003
### Website Goal

The website exists to generate enquiries, not to display statistics.

Reason:
The old SmartSport website focused on sports data. The new EireMetrics website should explain the service, demonstrate credibility and encourage clubs to contact us.

Status: Active

---

## Decision 004
### Primary Call To Action

Book a Free Match Analysis

Reason:
A free analysis lowers the barrier to entry and starts conversations with clubs.

Status: Active

---

## Decision 005
### Product Focus

Core services:

- Match Reports
- Team Dashboards
- Player Dashboards
- Performance Trends
- Opposition Analysis
- Season Reviews

Status: Active

---

## Decision 006
### Design Philosophy

The website should feel:

- Premium
- Modern
- Professional
- Fast
- Clean
- Sports focused

Avoid:
- Clutter
- Generic stock imagery
- Too many colours
- Long paragraphs

Status: Active
